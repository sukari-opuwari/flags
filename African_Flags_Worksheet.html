Grown up guide

African flags worksheet for ages 4 and up

Before you start

Print page 1 in colour at actual size. Use a thick pencil or crayon. Explain that these are four countries in Africa and that each country has its own flag.

Point to each flag in the picture key and say its country name. Invite the child to repeat it. Read one question at a time. Let the child compare the flags and circle an answer. Pointing is fine if drawing a circle is difficult.

Keep talking

The black star is on Ghana's flag. Botswana and Somalia both have blue on their flags.

Cover the picture key and try again when the child feels ready. Celebrate looking carefully and saying the names.

Adult support is expected. No independent reading or writing is needed.
